# Public Growth Audit — 2026-09-01

Scope: public/read-only evidence only. No Wix login, GBP mutation, campaign mutation, tracking mutation or spend.

## Website observations

Public homepage exposes Reservation, Order online and Delivery in the primary navigation. The homepage has a prominent Book a Table CTA and describes Parma through high-quality raw materials, relationships with Italian farmers, organic pizza and DE-ÖKO-007 certification.

The homepage section headed `Order Your Pizza Now` currently surfaces Wolt, Lieferando and Uber Eats. This creates a commercial tension with the stated business objective of increasing direct orders: the public homepage visibly sends ordering demand to marketplaces as well as the separate `Order online` path. Before changing anything, direct-order economics and the actual Wix ordering path should be measured separately from marketplace delivery.

Public reservation page `/reservations` exposes party size, date and time. This confirms a public first step but does not prove successful reservation completion or event semantics.

The public Delivery page explicitly presents Wolt, Lieferando and Uber Eats as partners.

## Public information consistency

Official Parma pages show opening hours Monday–Sunday, 17:00–23:00. Wanderlog and the current Restaurant Guru Parma listing also show all seven days 17:00–23:00. Falstaff currently shows Monday closed. Treat this as a local-presence consistency defect candidate, not as proof that customers are being lost.

The public Impressum contains visible spelling/quality defects including `Wangelstrße` instead of `Wrangelstraße` and several German-language typos. These are not measurement defects, but correcting them later would improve professionalism and entity consistency. No change is made here.

## Reputation/message evidence

Public review aggregators repeatedly surface:
- sourdough / distinctive dough;
- organic and high-quality ingredients;
- authentic Italian character;
- passionate/personal owner experience;
- vegan/vegetarian choice;
- occasional price sensitivity;
- historical complaints about waiting/service and at least one report of finding the restaurant closed despite published hours.

These are message hypotheses, not statistically representative sentiment unless backed by a complete review export.

## Growth implications

1. Direct-order traffic deserves its own measurement contract and value model; it must not be conflated with reservations or marketplace delivery.
2. The strongest defensible public positioning is not generic `pizza Berlin`: organic ingredients + sourdough/dough quality + Italian producer provenance + Kreuzberg/Wrangelkiez authenticity.
3. Price objections should be answered through value communication rather than discount assumptions.
4. Local-presence consistency (especially opening hours) should be audited before paid media scales local-intent traffic.
5. Mobile-first ordering/reservation remains a priority because Google Ads diagnostics show mobile overwhelmingly dominates campaign clicks.

## Deferred gates

- Wix direct-order funnel instrumentation: correct Wix access / deployed reader.
- Actual reservation and direct-order ground truth: correct account data.
- Directory/GBP edits: external write approval.
- Website copy/navigation edits: Wix access + explicit publication gate.
