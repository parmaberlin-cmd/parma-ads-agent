## Evidence strength

- **High for internal proposal use:** Parma-controlled facts already verified in the project (location, bio orientation, sourdough positioning, reservation/direct-order paths).
- **Medium:** public competitor positioning captured on 2026-09-01; suitable for hypothesis generation, not comparative factual advertising.
- **Low / unavailable:** competitor conversion rate, profitability, Google Ads strategy, true local-search rank, incremental customer value. These remain unknown.

## Message gap priorities

1. `Wrangelkiez / Kreuzberg` as the first relevance layer for local searches.
2. `Bio + Sauerteig` as a combined proof-oriented craft layer, rather than sourdough alone.
3. Specific verified ingredient/provenance proof instead of generic superlatives.
4. Separate calls to action for **visit**, **reserve**, and **order direct** so each can later receive its own measurement contract.
5. Do not use competitor names in ads or bid strategy merely because they appear in this research.

## Decision safety

This matrix can prioritize copy and measurement work. It cannot authorize competitor bidding, negative keywords, campaign writes, site publication, budget changes or spend. Any public comparative claim requires fresh source verification and separate approval.
