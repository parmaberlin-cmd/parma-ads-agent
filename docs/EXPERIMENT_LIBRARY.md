# Experiment Library

All entries are proposals only. No experiment may publish, mutate tracking or spend without its independent gate.

## EXP-G-001 — RSA structural rebuild
Hypothesis: a structurally complete RSA using Parma's defensible differentiators can improve ad relevance/engagement versus the current POOR 7-headline/2-description RSA.
Primary precondition: conversion integrity is not required to test structural/CTR signals, but business-outcome claims require verified measurement.
Guardrail: no automatic publication.
Rollback: restore prior RSA/assets.

## EXP-G-002 — Ad-group overlap cleanup
Hypothesis: reducing duplicate intent across ad groups can improve control and relevance for `beste pizza berlin`, `pizza napoletana berlin`, `pizza bio berlin`.
Precondition: exact keyword/match-type evidence and search-term routing review.
Guardrail: do not remove coverage based on unverified conversions.
Rollback: restore prior keyword/ad-group assignment.

## EXP-G-003 — Local-intent message
Hypothesis: Kreuzberg/Wrangelkiez + sourdough/organic provenance messaging better matches local high-intent searches than generic Berlin pizza copy.
Metrics: CTR/engagement first; verified customer value only after measurement integrity.

## EXP-SITE-001 — Direct-order CTA priority
Hypothesis: making Parma-controlled direct ordering more prominent than marketplace links increases direct-order share.
Preconditions: direct-order path verified, direct-order completion ground truth available, economics compared with marketplace orders.
Guardrail: do not hide marketplace options before measuring operational/customer impact.

## EXP-SITE-002 — Value communication
Hypothesis: explaining organic certification, sourdough, producer provenance and ingredient quality reduces price objection better than discounting.
Metrics: order/reservation completion and average order value when measurable.

## EXP-LOCAL-001 — Opening-hours consistency
Hypothesis: correcting stale third-party Monday-closed information reduces failed visits/confusion.
Precondition: official operating hours confirmed at time of edit.
This is an entity-consistency repair, not an Ads experiment.

## EXP-BUDGET-001 — Controlled budget step
Hypothesis: additional budget produces incremental verified customer value after measurement integrity and marginal response are established.
Candidate scenarios: 3.50 -> 5 -> 7 -> 10 EUR/day, never as an automatic staircase.
Preconditions: verified conversions/business value, mature comparison window, marginal response estimate.
Guardrail: spend authorization remains separate and mandatory.
