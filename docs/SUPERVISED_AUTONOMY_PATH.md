# Supervised Autonomy Path

## Stage 0 — Observe
Read-only collection, validation and diagnostics. No external mutation.

## Stage 1 — Recommend
Evidence-scoped recommendation with confidence, expected customer mechanism, risk, expiry and blocker class. Prediction is never permission.

## Stage 2 — Simulate
Reversible mutation package, rollback, success/failure thresholds and observation window. Still no external mutation.

## Stage 3 — Supervised reversible execution
Only after the applicable independent permission is present. Spend, security/payment, tracking, publication and other policy gates remain independent. Post-action verification is mandatory.

## Stage 4 — Learn
Record verified before/after evidence, external execution confirmation, observed outcome and whether the hypothesis was supported. Never convert an unexecuted proposal into learning evidence.

## Stage 5 — Bounded autonomy candidate
A class of action may become a candidate only after repeated verified outcomes, durable audit/history, kill switch, idempotency, rollback, fresh evidence and explicit delegation boundaries. Confidence alone cannot promote it.

## Non-promotable by inference
Budget/spend authority, credentials/security/payment actions, constitutional DNA changes and irreversible publication cannot be granted by model confidence or historical success alone.
