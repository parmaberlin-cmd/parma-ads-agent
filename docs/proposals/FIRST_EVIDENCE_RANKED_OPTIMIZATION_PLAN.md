# First Evidence-Ranked Parma Optimization Plan — no execution

Ranking is by expected incremental-customer mechanism, evidence strength, reversibility and risk. Current conversion counts are not trusted as business outcomes.

## Tier 1 — high evidence / low mutation risk
1. Repair measurement semantics and obtain Wix ground truth. Mechanism: prevents optimizing toward the wrong event. Impact: foundational. Execution: blocked by correct Wix access / publication gate where applicable.
2. Preserve local and near-me demand while measurement is incomplete. Mechanism: protects potential walk-ins. Evidence: near-me is the largest observed search-term cluster. No negative-keyword execution authorized.
3. Replace/rebuild structurally weak RSA in simulation. Mechanism: improve relevance/coverage without relying on conversion claims. Evidence: one RSA is structurally POOR versus a fuller GOOD RSA. Publication requires write gate.
4. Resolve public local-entity inconsistencies. Mechanism: reduce customer uncertainty about opening/location. Evidence: official all-days hours conflict with at least one public directory and the official Impressum has an address typo. External writes require approval/access.

## Tier 2 — strong demand evidence / outcome uncertain
5. De-cannibalize overlapping keyword/ad-group structure through a reversible experiment. Evidence: repeated high-volume keywords occur across both ad groups. Waste is not yet causally proven.
6. Local-intent message test: Kreuzberg/Wrangelkiez + verified product quality. Mechanism: better query-to-ad continuity for high-local-intent traffic.
7. Direct-order CTA experiment on mobile. Mechanism: shorten path from dominant mobile traffic to Parma-controlled order path. Requires correct site access and verified order measurement before judging value.
8. Mobile-first landing continuity review. Evidence: almost all Google clicks/cost are mobile.

## Tier 3 — potentially high impact, evidence gate required
9. Budget step scenarios 3.50→5→7→10 EUR/day. Current lost impression share shows constrained reach, but measurement/value and marginal response are unverified. Therefore no budget increase is recommended or authorized now.
10. Day/hour schedule experiments. Demand varies by hour, but conversion timing is not trusted; no schedule mutation until measurement integrity improves.
11. Geo refinement. Area-of-interest dominates observed traffic, but semantic/location-setting implications must be verified before changing targeting.

## Commercial decision rule
Prioritize verified incremental customer contribution, not conversion count. A proposal advances to execution only after: evidence fresh + measurement sufficiently verified + reversible mutation package + explicit applicable permission. Spend remains a separate gate.
