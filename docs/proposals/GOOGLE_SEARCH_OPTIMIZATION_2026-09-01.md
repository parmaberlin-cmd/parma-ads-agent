## Hypotheses to test

1. Rank is at least as important a constraint as budget; increasing budget before improving relevance/quality may buy more of the same traffic rather than better local demand.
2. The duplicate `beste pizza berlin` result is a useful natural comparison, but cannot yet be attributed to RSA quality alone; query mix, landing continuity and conversion tracking must be controlled first.
3. Mobile is the primary customer acquisition journey and should be the first UX/landing/booking diagnostic target.
4. Near-me queries may generate unmeasured directions, calls or walk-ins; zero registered booking conversions is insufficient evidence for exclusion.
