# Weekly Commercial Review Contract

The weekly review is customer-acquisition oriented, not an advertising vanity report.

## 1. Verified business outcomes
Report verified direct orders, verified reservations, marketplace orders and any separately verified walk-in evidence. Unknown values remain `unknown`.

## 2. Acquisition demand
Google impressions/clicks/CTR/CPC, intent-cluster mix, mobile share, local-intent share, impression-share constraints and public local-search changes. Registered platform conversions are labelled unverified until the Measurement Contract passes.

## 3. Commercial value
Contribution value, break-even CPA and LTV only where all required economics are verified. Never infer profitability from conversion volume.

## 4. What changed
Material week-over-week changes with freshness and maturity status. Separate observation, hypothesis and causal proof.

## 5. Experiments
For each proposal: evidence, expected incremental-customer mechanism, risk, reversibility, observation window, stop rule and required permission. No execution is implied.

## 6. Blockers
Separate software, data/maturity, external-access and permission blockers. A Wix or Meta access blocker must not make unrelated engineering work look blocked.

## 7. Next autonomous work
Select the highest-value GREEN task. Human gates are batched when safe; spend/payment/security/credential/irreversible gates remain independent.
